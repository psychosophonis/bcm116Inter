Beyond the single screen

These five step by steps provide you with the tools you'll need to explore multiscreen, 360, and multipanel (videos collaged on one screen) work. All of this also applies to projection work as well.

This week I am happy for you to work on last week's After Effects tutorial if you missed it. These exercises provide a nice extension to that - if three people make three sketches in After Effects (based perhaps on one theme, object or water colour) they will make a nice 3 screen triptych. If three people around you have made a nice After Effects spiral you can share that to learn how to place them in VR or in a grid or columns.

If the technicality of After Effects didn't inspire you or you want to move on, grab some others and shoot some video you can use to test this stuff. Look at the student examples as clues or come up with an idea that explores a relation between video frames: people talking across the frames, singing together in the round, passing an object or a note, or simply engaging in the same activity. Perhaps it’s even just a static video portrait.

Here are examples from BCM116 assessments that played with this idea:

  • The Eccentric Eulogy, Aidan Buckley and Kobey Hill (2025): a comic funeral staged in 360°. You turn from one speaker to the next.
  • Disparate Diaspora, Marotetini Tehei (2024): three versions of herself argue across three screens, each in her own language.
  • Walk this way, a tutorial exercise: everyone filmed their own feet walking, shown side by side as strips.

If it helps, think about where that idea might end up: as video arranged in VR space, as a grid on a single screen, or across two or three screens. Choose your step-by-step accordingly, or try various forms and see how they suggest different projects from the same content.

The aim whatever you do is look for potential project ideas for 3 & 4.

AssessmentInvestigation 3 & 4

The challenge for investigation 3 & 4 is to get started early - you can make 2, 2 min videos at 20% each or make a longer 4 min work marked at 40%. As with investigation 2 I'm happy for you to propose group works that double length or triple length. This would allow you to make a single work across three screens or 4 minutes run time with a team of three (for example). Maximum of three to a group. Submit 1 didactic panel per group.

If you're planning on exploring projection or an installation work plan to have it ready for install in week 13 or make plans with Mat to install for marking on Tuesday of week 14.
