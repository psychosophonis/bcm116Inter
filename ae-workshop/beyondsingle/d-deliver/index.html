Preparing your work for the screen array

Here’s what you’ll make. One ProRes file, 11520 × 2160, ready to play on the lab’s array of three 4K screens. It’s always this size, even if your work uses only two screens.

Why we’re doing this. The array plays one file across all three screens, so every work arrives at the same size. You build each screen in HD, because 4K is heavy to edit, and the last step doubles each one to fill its 4K screen.

What you’ll come away with. How to put your screens side by side in a 4K Master, export it as ProRes, and hand it in.

Start with one 1920 × 1080 video for each screen. That might be the Screen sequences from Two or more screens, loops from the After Effects workshop, or HD videos you’ve made somewhere else.

Part 1Make the 4K Master in Premiere

D1 · Master and export in Premiere 3 steps

  1. D-01

    Choose File → New → Sequence: Custom, Square Pixels, Frame Size 11520 × 2160, and the same Timebase as your screens (30 for phone video and your After Effects loops). Name it Master.

    Check the width stuck. Choose Sequence → Sequence Settings. If the width now says 10240, your version of Premiere won’t make a sequence this wide: use After Effects instead.
    Don’t leave the Timebase at the default. Premiere suggests 23.976. If your screens are 30, Premiere has to drop frames to fit them in.
    Why 11520 × 2160? The array is three 4K screens, each 3840 × 2160, and the file lays them side by side.
  2. D-02

    Drag your screens (the Screen 1, Screen 2 and Screen 3 sequences, or your three videos) from the Project panel into the Master sequence, each on its own track. Select each one in the timeline and, in the Properties panel, set its Scale to 200 and its Position to 1920, 5760 or 9600 across (and 1080 down), left to right.

    Making two-screen work? Place two screens only, at 1920 and 5760. Leave the third panel empty: it exports as black, and that screen stays dark.
    Asked about a clip mismatch? Choose Keep existing settings, so the Master stays at 11520 × 2160.
    Where do these numbers come from? Scale 200 doubles each HD screen to 3840 × 2160, exactly one 4K screen. Position is the centre, so the centres are 3840 apart, starting at 1920 (half of 3840).
    Master playing slowly? Set the Program Monitor’s playback resolution (the dropdown at the bottom right) to 1/4. It only changes the preview, not the export.
  3. ✓

    Check before you go on. The screens should sit side by side, edge to edge, with no gaps or overlaps. Three screens fill the wide frame; two fill the left two-thirds.

  4. D-03

    Choose File → Export → Media, set Format to QuickTime and Preset to Apple ProRes 422 Proxy, and export. Then go to Hand it in.

Part 2Or make the 4K Master in After Effects

The same Master, built as an After Effects composition. Use this if Premiere won’t keep the 11520 width, or if your screens came from the After Effects workshop. If your screens are Premiere sequences, export each one as an HD file first.

Using DaVinci Resolve? The free version caps timelines and exports at 3840 × 2160, so it can’t make the Master (Resolve Studio, the paid version, can). Edit your screens in Resolve, export each one as a 1920 × 1080 file, and build the Master in Premiere or After Effects.

D2 · Master and export in After Effects 3 steps

  1. D-04

    Choose Composition → New Composition. Untick Lock Aspect Ratio, then set Width 11520, Height 2160, Pixel Aspect Ratio Square Pixels, Frame Rate to match your screens (30), Duration to the length of your work, and Background Color to black. Name it Master.

  2. D-05

    Import your three HD videos and drag them into the Master. For each layer, press S and set Scale to 200, then press P and set Position to 1920, 1080, 5760, 1080 or 9600, 1080, left to right.

    Making two-screen work? Place two videos only. The empty third panel renders as the black background.
  3. D-06

    Choose Composition → Add to Render Queue. Click the Output Module setting, set Format to QuickTime, then under Format Options set Video Codec to Apple ProRes 422 Proxy. Choose where to save, and click Render.

Part 3Hand it in

D3 · Check and deliver 1 step

  1. ✓

    Check before you hand it in. Open the exported file and skim through it. Each screen’s video should sit in its own third of the frame, from start to finish.

  2. D-07

    Give the ProRes file to me directly, or upload it to the drop box. The link is on Moodle and Discord.

    Expect a big file. ProRes Proxy at this size is roughly 4 GB a minute. Check you have the space before you export, and allow time for the upload.
